What format are the digital products in?

All products are delivered as downloadable PDFs. They are printable and optimized for both screen reading and physical use. The checklists include interactive checkboxes that work in most PDF readers.

Are these products updated for 2026?

Yes. All products reflect current 2026 USCIS fees, processing times, form versions, and embassy procedures. Core products include 12 months of updates for the covered materials.

Do I get updates?

Yes. The current products include 12 months of updates for the covered materials. If you still need reminders and refreshed downloads after that period, the Update Pass is the recurring layer.

Can I use these products if I have an attorney?

Absolutely. Many of our customers use these alongside their attorney. The document checklist helps you gather everything before your attorney meeting, saving billable hours. The interview prep guide covers questions your attorney may not have time to rehearse with you.

The document checklist covers the top 10 K-1 visa countries: Philippines, Mexico, Colombia, Brazil, Dominican Republic, Vietnam, India, China, Ukraine, and the United Kingdom. The interview prep guide includes embassy-specific tips for the Philippines, Mexico, Colombia, India, and general guidance applicable to all embassies.

The free checklist is a simplified overview. The paid Complete Document Checklist includes country-specific variations, "what to do if missing" guidance, detailed document descriptions, and covers every stage from I-129F through AOS. It is significantly more comprehensive.
